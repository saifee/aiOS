# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability
Email **security@YOUR-DOMAIN** (replace with your real contact before publishing) with a description, reproduction steps and impact. Please do not open public issues for vulnerabilities. We aim to acknowledge within 3 business days and to fix critical issues within 72 hours (see `docs/security/VULNERABILITY_MANAGEMENT.md` for the full SLA).

## Supported versions
Only the latest release on `main` receives security fixes.

## Scope & safe harbour
In scope: the aiOS API, web app and bundled services in this repository. Out of scope: social engineering, denial-of-service load tests, third-party providers (Hostinger, Twilio, Meta, AI vendors).

## Where to read more
`docs/security/` — overview, threat model, risk register, standards mapping, secure deployment (Hostinger), incident response. These documents describe controls the project implements and tests; they are **not** a compliance certification.
